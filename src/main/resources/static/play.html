    How to play
    • On your turn, dots show where you can move. Click one.
    • Pick an attack (or press its number), then click an orange square. Hover first to preview: red is damage, blue arrows show pushes and pulls.
    • Attacks that hit everything around you (Push Back, Pull, Queen lines) fire when you click your hero, anyone they affect, or the button again.
    • ★ buttons are Supers. A dashed gold ring means a hero's Super is ready.
    • The gold diamond is the Keystone. The green dot means poisoned.
    • Esc cancels, M goes back to moving, Enter ends your turn.
    • Bombs are hidden from everyone. Remember where they went off.
